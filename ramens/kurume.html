Kurume Ramen

「久留米ラーメン — The origin of modern tonkotsu, intense and full of history.」

Kurume Ramen is the root of modern tonkotsu ramen. Its thick broth is prepared with pork bones simmered for more than 12 hours, resulting in an intense and cloudy flavor.

Ingredients

  • Pork bone broth
  • Thin noodles
  • Chashu pork, fried garlic and scallions
  • Light soy sauce

Flavor

Deep, salty and with smoky nuances.

Scent

Rich, intense and meaty.

Texture

Thick and silky, with a heavy, comforting body.
