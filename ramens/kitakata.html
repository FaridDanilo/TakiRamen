Kitakata Ramen

「喜多方ラーメン — Light, balanced and with a taste of tradition.」

Kitakata Ramen comes from the Fukushima region. It is distinguished by its clear shoyu broth and thick, wavy noodles. It's one of the most balanced and simple ramen styles.

Ingredients

  • Shoyu-based broth with pork and fish stock
  • Thick, wavy noodles
  • Menma bamboo shoots, pork, scallion and egg

Flavor

Delicate yet deep.

Scent

Light with notes of soy and dried fish.

Texture

Soft but with good chewiness.
