Taiwan Ramen

「台湾ラーメン — Energy, spices and eastern tradition in a single bowl.'

Taiwan Ramen is a Japanese creation inspired by Taiwanese cuisine. Spicy and bold, this ramen combines ground meat, garlic and chiles, resulting in a powerful and flavorful bowl.

Ingredients

  • Chicken or pork-based broth
  • Ground meat sautéed with garlic and chili
  • Medium noodles
  • Scallion and bean sprouts

Flavor

Spicy, umami and slightly sweet.

Scent

Intense with notes of garlic and chili.

Texture

Light but powerful, with just the right spice.
